The maternal left endpoint is unphased at GQ 9. At the right endpoint, the VCF assigns A to maternal HP2, while all eight eligible maternal reads tagged HP2 in that phase set carry G. The three child reads assigned maternal origin by the baseline carry C/A, and their four-site C/C/A/A pattern also occurs in four maternal reads and one paternal read.
