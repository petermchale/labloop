The trio reads support a paternal HP1-to-HP2 switch in NA12879 within NA12877 phase set 19256651. Eight child reads assigned to paternal origin carry G/G across the endpoints, four on each strand. Six directly carry the paternal allele at the Mendelian anchor at 20,955,483. The father has 17 HP1 G/A reads and 17 HP2 A/G reads spanning the endpoints, and neither parent has an eligible G/G spanning read. All eight G/G child reads link to upstream HP1 markers, three also link to downstream HP2 markers, and two cover both 20,968,297 and 20,968,600. The IGV overview shows child alignments extending from the interval into the downstream marker region. The reproduced BAM and VCF audit supports the switch at marker resolution.
